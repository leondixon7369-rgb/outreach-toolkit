Our Story

From a single van to the region's trusted builder

Max Gibb started the business back in 2004 with one van, a set of tools and a simple belief: do honest work, treat people fairly, and the rest looks after itself.

Twenty years and 350+ projects later, that belief hasn't changed. We've grown into a skilled, close-knit team — but we've kept the same hands-on, personal approach that earned us our reputation in the first place.

When you work with us, you're not a job number. You get a builder who picks up the phone, turns up when he says he will, and treats your home like his own.
