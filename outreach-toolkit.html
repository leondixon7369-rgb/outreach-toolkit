📞 Cold Call — Opening

The first 10 seconds decide everything. Be quick, be human.

"Hi, is that [Name]? ... Hi [Name], my name's [Your Name] — I'll be quick, I know you're busy. I build websites for [plumbers/roofers] round [town], and I redesigned yours as a free demo to show you what it could look like. Is it alright if I text or email you the link to have a look? No charge, no catch."

If they say yes:

"Brilliant — what's the best number or email? ... Perfect, I'll fire it over now. Have a look when you get two minutes, and if you like it I can have the full thing live this week. If not, no worries at all — keep the demo."

Send the demo immediately while on the call or right after.

🛡️ Objection Handling

"Why do I need this? I get all my work word of mouth."

"Honestly, that's the best kind of work to have — and I'm not trying to replace it. Here's the thing though: when someone recommends you, the first thing the new customer does is Google you before they ring. If they find nothing, or an old site, some of them hesitate or go with the next name. A proper site just makes sure the referrals you're already getting actually pick up the phone — it backs up your reputation instead of letting it leak. That's all it does."

Don't attack word-of-mouth. Reframe the site as plugging a leak in referrals they already get.

"How much is it?"

"Setup's [£450–600] one-off, then [£50] a month to host it, keep it running and make any changes you need. But genuinely — have a look at the demo first, no point talking money until you've seen if you even like it."

"I already have someone who does my website."

"No problem at all. Mind if I send the demo anyway? Costs you nothing to compare — if yours is already better, you'll know in ten seconds and I'll leave you be."

"I'm not interested / haven't got time."

"Totally fair, I'll not keep you. I'll just text the demo link over so it's there if you ever fancy a look — sound alright? Cheers [Name], take care."

"Is this a scam / who are you again?"

"Ha, fair question — no, I'm local, I build sites for trades round here. There's nothing to buy on the phone. I just made you a free demo and wanted you to see it. I'll text it from this number now so you've got my details."

💬 Follow-Up Texts

Right after the call:

Hi [Name], [Your Name] here — great speaking with you. Here's the free demo I made for your site: [link]

Have a look when you get a sec. Happy to tweak anything or get it live this week if you like it 👍

Next day (no reply):

Hi [Name], did the demo come through ok? No rush — just shout if you've any questions or want any changes made.

3–4 days later (then leave it):

Hey [Name], I'll leave you in peace after this! The demo's yours to keep either way — link's here again if it's handy: [link]. Cheers 🙂

✅ Two rules that make it convert

1. Lead with the demo, never the pitch. "I built you something free" gets a yes; "can I sell you a website" gets a no.

2. Follow up. Aim for ~30 touches a day. Most sales happen on the 2nd–4th contact, not the first — that's what the tracker is for.
